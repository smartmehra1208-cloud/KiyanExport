About Kiyan Export

Kiyan Export, incepted in 2005, gradually evolved from a spice-trading company into a trusted worldwide exporter of herbal supplements and natural wellness products. We operate on the very basic principles of high-quality work, trustworthiness, and India's ancient heritage of herbal practices, focusing extensively on third-party production and private labeling services for wellness brands, startups, and companies across the globe.

Herbal capsules, powders, oils, resins, teas, and natural supplements are prepared from ethically sourced ingredients and manufactured in certified factories following stringent quality standards.

Location: Gomti Nagar, Lucknow, India (with additional international coordination networks).

Leadership: Headed by CEO Aparna Batra.

Core Products: Herbal powders, bamboo salt, natural herbal extracts, copper products, and traditional brass items like urli.

Services: Supplier, wholesaler, and global exporter offering natural wellness products and private labeling/bulk distribution.

Kiyan Export offers a seamless one-window solution from product development to packaging and onwards to international delivery. It is recognized across the globe for timely delivery and for fostering business relationships, especially in exports to the USA, Germany, France, Switzerland, Ireland, Brazil, Turkey, and Romania.
