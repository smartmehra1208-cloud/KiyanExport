WHO WE ARE

Uncompromising Herbal Purity Direct To You

Established in 2014 in Lucknow, India, Kiyan Wellness was founded with a clear vision: to make authentic, lab-certified organic herbal remedies accessible directly to health-conscious retail consumers everywhere.

As a specialized B2C wellness company, Kiyan Wellness offers a signature collection of pure Himalayan Shilajit, KSM-66 Ashwagandha, Moringa, Bacopa, Sea Moss, organic herbal powders, gummies, and natural health care essentials.

Our certified processing facilities follow strict ISO 9001 and FSSAI standards, ensuring third-party lab verification for heavy metal safety, active ingredient potency, and 100% natural purity.

Our Retail Mission

To deliver 100% pure, potent, and ethically sourced herbal supplements directly to households, helping people live healthier lives naturally.

Our Brand Vision
